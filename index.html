About Us

Greetings! Welcome to our webpage. We are proud students of the 12th-grade Computer Science batch (2024-25) at B.K. Birla College of Arts, Science & Commerce.

This page is dedicated to our beloved teachers by the students of 12th D, as a token of gratitude for their guidance, inspiration, and unwavering support throughout our journey.

Discover the heartfelt moments preserved in our farewell slideshow, where every image is a labor of love and dedication. We sincerely appreciate you joining us on this memorable journey!

Event Report

As the sun set on our school days, we, the students of Class 12th D Computer Science, came together for a meaningful and heartwarming celebration on the final day of our college journey. It wasn’t just a farewell; it was a legacy—a gesture of love and gratitude for the institution and the environment that nurtured us.

The idea of the sapling plantation drive was conceived with a vision to leave behind a mark that would grow and flourish, much like the memories we created here. Planning for the event began well in advance. In coordination with a local nursery, we selected saplings to symbolize growth, hope, and sustainability. Our beloved Computer Science teacher, Omkar Sir, went above and beyond by managing the purchase and transportation of the saplings during our exam schedule. Meanwhile, the chief gardener, Mr. Chaku, meticulously planned every detail, identifying the perfect spots in the biodiversity park where the saplings would thrive.

The day began on a nostalgic note as we gathered in the Physics laboratory, a place that had witnessed our journey from eager learners to confident individuals. The college, in its thoughtful gesture, arranged refreshing snacks for us, fostering an atmosphere of camaraderie. It was a brief yet precious moment of togetherness, one that reminded us of the countless breaks we shared over the years.

At 3:00 PM, brimming with excitement and a shared sense of purpose, we made our way to the biodiversity park. The venue, lush and serene, seemed to echo our emotions —a blend of anticipation for the future and bittersweet goodbyes. We were honored to be graced by the presence of our esteemed dignitaries: Director Sir, Principal Sir, Vice Principal Ma’am, and Additional Vice-Principal Ma’am. Their words of wisdom resonated deeply within us, inspiring us to carry the values instilled in us by the institution into the world beyond.

Together with Azima Ma’am and Ashish Sir, we sang motivational songs that uplifted our spirits and reinforced the bond we shared. Each sapling planted symbolized a dream, a memory, a promise to contribute to the world in meaningful ways.
