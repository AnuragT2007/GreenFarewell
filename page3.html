The Papaya

Discover the beauty, significance, and ecological benefits of the Papaya.

About Papaya

The papaya (*Carica papaya*) is a tropical fruit tree known for its sweet taste, vibrant orange flesh, and numerous health benefits. It is native to Central and South America and is often called the "fruit of angels."

Planted By: Ms. Vaishnavi and Ms. Nitya

Event Name: GreenFarewell 2025

Scientific Name

Species: Carica papaya

Family: Caricaceae

Varieties

  • Hawaiian Papaya: Smaller, sweeter, with orange-red flesh.
  • Mexican Papaya: Larger, with yellow or orange flesh, used for cooking.
  • Mountain Papaya (Vasconcellea pubescens): Grown in cooler climates, used in juices and jams.

Nutritional Value (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 43 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 11 g
  • Dietary Fiber: 1.7 g
  • Vitamin C: 60% of the daily recommended intake
  • Vitamin A: 20% of the daily recommended intake
  • Folate: 10% of the daily recommended intake
  • Rich in antioxidants like beta-carotene and lycopene.

Health Benefits

  • Digestive Aid: Contains papain enzyme for better digestion.
  • Rich in Antioxidants: Protects cells and boosts immunity.
  • Heart Health: Promotes cardiovascular health.
  • Improves Skin Health: Reduces acne and promotes glowing skin.
  • Anti-inflammatory: Reduces inflammation and aids muscle recovery.

Uses

  • Culinary: Fresh, in smoothies, curries, and desserts. Seeds are used as a spice.
  • Medicinal: Used for treating digestive issues and wounds.
  • Cosmetic: Common in face masks, scrubs, and soaps.
  • Industrial: Papain is used in tenderizing meat and clarifying beer.

Growing Conditions

  • Climate: Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates.
  • Soil: Well-drained soil with a pH of 6.0–6.5.
  • Sunlight: Requires full sun for optimal growth.
  • Watering: Regular watering but avoid waterlogging.
  • Care Tips: Sensitive to cold and needs spacing for air circulation.

Interesting Facts

  • Papaya is botanically a berry.
  • It can produce fruit all year round in suitable climates.
  • Green papaya is rich in latex and used for natural remedies.
  • Christopher Columbus called it the “fruit of angels.”
